# Silverstone Reborn Modpack
Silverstone Reborn is an ambitious passion project that aims to recreate and perfect the original Silverstone modpack experience — a piece of content that eventually became lost media.
Rather than just being a collection of mods, it’s a carefully curated world with its own atmosphere, presentation, and lore-inspired identity.

## Overview
Silverstone Reborn blends exploration, survival, and immersive visual design into a sandbox tailored for players who want something beyond vanilla Minecraft. Every aspect — from graphics to audio — has been hand-tuned to capture the “feel” of a standalone game while still being Minecraft at its core.

Custom Soundtrack: Featuring all main menu tracks, including Aurora Veil, to set the tone the moment you launch.

Distinct Menus: Redesigned Singleplayer and Multiplayer menus with unique background art.

Performance-Aware Visuals: Pre-configured medium-high settings for smooth visuals, with shaders and effects easily adjustable or disabled by the player.

Balanced Gameplay Additions: Enriched survival mechanics and world immersion without overwhelming the vanilla core.

## Multiplayer
Multiplayer exists but is experimental — currently LAN-based. Players can connect via external tunneling software (Playit.gg, Hamachi, Radmin, etc.) but stability is not guaranteed. The singleplayer experience remains the most stable and polished way to play.

# Installation Guide
## Step 1
Install the [Modrinth App](https://modrinth.com) and connect to your Microsoft account
## Step 2
Click on the plus icon on the left side of the screen to add an instance
![step2photo](https://github.com/kovrmyface/Silverstone-Reborn/blob/releases/Silverstone-guide.png?raw=true)
## Step 3
Choose the "**From File**" option and click on "**Import from file**"
![step3photo](https://github.com/kovrmyface/Silverstone-Reborn/blob/releases/Silverstone-guide2.png?raw=true)
## Step 4
Select your Silverstone Reborn installation that has a **.mrpack** extension
![step4photo](https://github.com/kovrmyface/Silverstone-Reborn/blob/releases/Silverstone-guide3.png?raw=true)
## Step 5
Wait for everything to download. After that, click on the instance that just downloaded and click on the **Play** button
![step5photo](https://github.com/kovrmyface/Silverstone-Reborn/blob/releases/Silverstone-guide4.png?raw=true)
